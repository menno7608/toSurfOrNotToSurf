Archimedes OS

A personal operating system for one person's notes, mail and calendar.

Archimedes OS is private software written and run by Menno Boot. It runs on a Raspberry Pi at his home and has exactly one user: its owner. It is not offered to anyone else, has no sign-up, and collects nothing from visitors of this page.

What it does

  • Collects the owner's own notes, voice recordings and reading material into one searchable store on his own hardware.
  • Shows which mail arrived and which appointments are coming up, so the day fits on one screen.
  • Prepares proposals that the owner approves or rejects himself; the system never acts on his behalf without that approval.

Which Google data it reads

  • gmail.readonly — the sender's domain and the date of recent mail. Subjects and message bodies are never requested.
  • calendar.readonly — the titles and times of upcoming events.
  • calendar.events — adds an event to the owner's calendar, only after the owner has approved that event.

Mail is read-only, and everything stays on the owner's own devices. The details are in the privacy policy.
